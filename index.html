How to play

  1. Step on the ⚡ switch to open the 🚧 gate, grab the 🔑 key, then reach the 🚪 exit.
  2. You have 3 ❤️ lives. A monster hit costs one and respawns you safely. Lose them all and you die.
  3. Monsters: 🟢 chasers (slow, chase when close), 🦇 bats (fast, random), 👹 guardians (protect gems), and a boss on level 5.
  4. Face a monster and press Space to throw a 🪨 rock (stuns) or 💣 bomb (kills, hurts the boss).
  5. Find the 🔴 red key to open the vault door. Cracked walls hide secret rooms: walk into them.
  6. Collect coins and gems quickly for a combo multiplier. Collect all of them for a +50 treasure bonus.
